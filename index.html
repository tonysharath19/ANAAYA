Our Story

Jewellery Should Not
Wait For An OCCASSION!

DASHA began with a simple belief: you should not need a wedding invitation to wear jewellery that feels extraordinary. We design artificial fashion jewellery inspired by India’s rich craft traditions, reimagined for the modern wardrobe.

Every piece is crafted from high-quality brass alloys, gold-tone and rhodium-tone finishes, CZ-style stones, and carefully sourced artificial pearls and glass beads. Statement jewellery, honestly priced.
